Deadbeat Records is a fictional independent record shop built around a simple idea: some music deserves your whole attention.

Take your time.

Our point of view

A record is a place to return to.

We made Deadbeat for the listeners who read the liner notes, leave the phone in another room, and let one side finish before choosing the next. Our imagined shelves bring together new voices, familiar sounds, and records that reward a second listen.

Every title in this collection is fictional, selected to show how a quiet, considered record store might feel online. No rush. No endless scroll. Just music, chosen with care.

What matters here

Three simple things

01

Listen closely.

Good curation starts with paying attention. We choose records for the details that make you come back.

02

Keep it tangible.

Artwork, notes, the small ritual of lowering a needle. The object is part of the experience.

03

Make room.

For unexpected sounds, new perspectives, and the record you didn’t know you were looking for.
